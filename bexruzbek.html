🎙️ HOW TO GET AN 8 IN IELTS SPEAKING
🗣️

Fluency & Coherence

Speak without long pauses. Use discourse markers ("actually", "moreover", "to be honest"). Never memorise scripts – link ideas naturally.

📚

Lexical Resource

Show advanced vocabulary & paraphrasing. Avoid repetition – say "exhilarating" instead of "good". Use idiomatic language (spot on, a piece of cake).

🎯

Grammar Range

Use complex structures: conditionals, inversion, relative clauses. Mix tenses naturally. "Had I known, I would have acted differently."

🎧

Pronunciation

Master word stress & intonation. Record yourself. Use connected speech. Band 8 requires clarity AND natural rhythm — not a robotic accent.

🏆 BAND 8 FORMULA ✨ Expand each answer (20-30 sec minimum) + give examples + express opinions. Never answer "yes/no" without elaboration. Be confident and smile! ✨

💡 Mock exam trick: simulate real conditions, use IELTS speaking topics (travel, education, technology). Get feedback from advanced speakers or AI.
